## 1. Why Open Arbor Moved from `RCA` to `RAP` and `RAPPER` (Protocol-First Ontology)

Open Arbor began exploring credential design while evaluating the American Society of Consulting Arborists (`ASCA`) Registered Consulting Arborist credential (`bodhi/rca-viability-deep-analysis-2026-10-18`, qualified in `bodhi/post-session-sync-2026-10-18-currentness-audit`), and briefly used the internal label `"Regenerative Consulting Arborist (RCA)"`. On 2026-11-12 (commit `9ec1690`), Open Arbor retired `"Regenerative Consulting Arborist (RCA)"` in [docs/13_RCA_PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_v1.md](./13_RCA_PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_v1.md) and cut over to [docs/13_RAP_PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_v1.md](./13_RAP_PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_v1.md) (`RAP-Spec v2.0`) for three reasons:

1. **Statutory and Trademark Clarity:** In North American arboriculture and municipal permitting, `RCA` denotes a Registered Consulting Arborist credentialed under `ASCA` (`ASCA_Registered_Consulting_Arborist` in `schemas/rap_openbadge_v3.schema.json`). Open Arbor reserves `RCA` strictly for `ASCA` Registered Consulting Arborist references so municipal reviewers, attorneys, and working arborists never confuse open diagnostic records with a private guild title.
2. **Protocol Over Caste:** Naming the project after a credential title centered a closed professional caste instead of an open methodology. Centering the **Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol (`RAP`)** makes the deterministic rules, biomechanics, and schemas primary (`AGPL-3.0-only` engine code under `src/`, `bin/`, `web/`, and `server/`; `CC0-1.0` public-domain JSON schemas and ordinance datasets under `schemas/` and `data/`; and `CC BY-SA 4.0` practice prose under `docs/`). Practitioners who demonstrate verifiable field outcomes against `RAP-Spec v2.0` earn the **`RAPPER`** designation rather than purchasing gatekept status.
3. **Backward Compatibility Without Collision:** Thin legacy shims keep earlier URLs, exports, and integrations operational while the canonical surface stays pure. Legacy shims remain active at `src/core/rcaEngine.js`, `src/core/leoRcaEngine.js`, `bin/openarbor-rca.mjs`, `schemas/rca_assessment.schema.json`, `schemas/rca_openbadge_v3.schema.json`, MCP tool aliases (`rca_biomechanics`, `rca_lint`, `leo_rca_verify`), and the `#oa1=` state permalink `.rca` JSON envelope key. Legacy discriminators `RAP_v1`, `RCA_v1`, and `RCA_v2` remain schema-compatible alongside canonical `RAP_v2`.

## 2. From Consulting Arborist to Practitioner in Ecological Restoration (`RAPPER`)

Renaming the practitioner from `"Consulting Arborist"` to **`Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol Practitioner in Ecological Restoration`** aligns credential identity with biological reality. Managing mature woody perennials as nested holobionts across a 200-year horizon (`STIM` Protocol v7.0011, `DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21297458`) requires connecting aboveground trunk mechanics to rhizosphere porosity, vadose hydrology, fungal inoculation, and native understory plant guilds. This broader mandate maps across the five mandatory domains in `schemas/rap_assessment.schema.json` and Open Arbor's seven core ESM modules (`src/core/`):

* **Aboveground Biomechanics and Root Protection (`src/core/rootZone.js`, `src/core/rapEngine.js`):** Implements `ANSI A300` (Part 5), `ISA BMP`, `ISA TRAQ`, Australian `AS 4970-2009` Structural Root Zone (`SRZ`) windthrow math, UK `BS 5837:2012`, Alex Shigo's `CODIT` decay compartmentalization model, Claus Mattheck's hollow-cylinder bending safety threshold (`t/R >= 0.30`), and `1-ft` spatial grid union de-duplication (`unionRootZoneArea`) so overlapping grove root zones are not double-counted.
* **Pneumatic Rhizosphere Remediation (`src/core/rapEngine.js`):** Computes radial pneumatic excavation (`150 psi` AirSpade radial trenching), stem-girdling root (`SGR`) relief, root-flare exposure, and biological amendments scaled to the Tree Influence Zone (`TIZ`): pre-charged biochar (`8%` of decompacted volume), fungal compost (`15%` of decompacted volume), humate (`1.5 kg/m3`), and coarse ramial woodchip mulch (`8 cm` depth across `TIZ`).
* **Vadose Soil Hydrology (`src/core/soilFunction.js`):** Captures bulk density (`g/cm3`), penetrometer resistance (`psi`), infiltration rate (`mm/hr`), soil organic matter (`organic_matter_percent`), and soil pH baseline alongside `validateSoilFunction` provenance checks (`measurement_dates` ISO pairs, `source`, and `measured` vs. `estimated` method flags) and `estimateSoilSpongeCapacity` (computed from NRCS soil-water capacity benchmarks of roughly `20,000 gal/acre` per `1%` organic matter above a `1%` baseline across the top `6 in`, plus `25.4 mm` storm volume capture across `TIZ`, classified into `Compacted (< 15 mm/hr)`, `Moderate (15 to 50 mm/hr)`, and `Living Sponge (> 50 mm/hr)`).
* **Bioregional Guilds, Allometrics, and Municipal Law (`src/core/ordinanceEngine.js`, `src/core/ecosystemBenefits.js`):** Encodes declarative municipal ordinances (`central_texas_ordinances.json` covering Austin, Georgetown, Liberty Hill, Cedar Park, and Round Rock; `rogue_valley_oregon_ordinances.json` covering Ashland, Medford, and Grants Pass), enforces seasonal *Ceratocystis fagacearum* (Oak Wilt) vector restrictions, pairs restoration prescriptions with Society for Ecological Restoration (`SER`) reference ecosystems and iNaturalist-tagged native companion species guilds (`inaturalist_taxon_id`), exports `OGC CRS84` GeoJSON (`toGeoJSONFeatureCollection`), and tracks modeled carbon, rainfall interception, and cooling benefits (`Nowak 1994`, `Cairns et al. 1997`, `McPherson & Simpson 1999`, `Xiao et al. 2000`, `Nowak et al. 2006`, and `John White FCIN12`).

## 3. Dual Semiotics of `RAP` and `"That's a RAP"`

Inside Project Open Arbor, `RAP` carries two coupled operational meanings supported directly in engine validation and prompt architecture:

1. **Physical Acoustic Rapping and Instrument Provenance:** When assessing a hollow trunk in the field, arborists tap the bark perimeter with a sounding mallet (*rapping* the outer shell) to listen for resonance changes before testing suspected cavities with resistance micro-drilling (`resistograph`) or acoustic velocity tomography (`sonic_tomography`). Open Arbor encodes a strict line between acoustic screening impressions and instrument quantification: `composeArboristHandoffBrief` separates `sonicTomographyVerified: true` from hypothesis defaults, and `fieldObservationLog.js` (`baseline.internal_decay_method`) enforces explicit `null` for `baseline.measured_t_over_r` whenever `visual_only_unmeasured` is recorded (`PROVENANCE_VIOLATION` if any numeric `t/R` is attached without instrument measurement).
2. **Linguistic Clarity Through `LEO-RAP` Profile 01:** `src/core/leoRapEngine.js` (`compileLeoRapBrief`, `verifyLeoRapNarrative`, `runLeoRapSelfAudit`) compiles deterministic 6-slot Logical English Orchestration (`LEO-RAP`) briefs aligned with the `openarbor-steward` skill (`agents/openarbor-steward/SKILL.md`). It adapts tone across `homeowner` (converting DBH to chest-height circumference at `4.5 ft` and omitting unverified `t/R` defaults), `practitioner` (citing `ANSI A300`, `AS 4970`, Mattheck `t/R >= 0.30`, AirSpade volume, and `STIM` falsifiers), and `municipal` (citing exact code chapters and elevating `UNVERIFIED` statutory subsections) audiences while locking every key number to `[[oracle:key=value]]` anchors and verifying self-audit defenses against 3 negative sabotage fixtures (`RAP_MATTHECK_T_OVER_R`, `MAST_FM_2_6`, and `EPISTEMIC_STATUS_DRIFT`).
3. **Evidentiary Closure (`"That's a RAP"`):** When `lintRapEvidentiaryReport` passes all five mandatory gates (`MISSING_DOMAIN`, `INVALID_BIOMETRICS` / `INVALID_GEO_LOCATION`, `UNJUSTIFIED_REMOVAL` / `EVIDENTIARY_DEFECT`, `OAK_WILT_VIOLATION`, and `INCOMPLETE_SOIL_DATA`) and `compileRapEvidentiaryMarkdown` seals the record with `computeCanonicalSha256`, an un-evidenced removal recommendation hits a verifiable biomechanical and statutory stop via `compilePreservationCounterNotice`.

## 4. Four Architectural Pillars in Open Arbor `v2.1.0`

Open Arbor (`v2.1.0`, `openarbor-mcp` `v2.2.0`) implements the `RAP` and `RAPPER` model across four concrete engineering pillars:

* **Pillar 1: Collaborative Arborist Recruitment and Phased Stewardship Triage (`src/core/leoRapEngine.js`):** Rather than mailing confrontational counter-notices on initial outreach, `composeArboristHandoffBrief` separates client-side handoffs from practitioner benchmarks. `steward_to_arborist_message` intentionally omits synthetic `t/R` assumptions and contractor bid prices (`stage1Usd`, `removalBidUsd`), sending only measured DBH and circumference, species, municipal protection tier, CRZ radius, cash-in-lieu mitigation exposure, active seasonal Oak Wilt restrictions, an on-site inspection request, and the `#oa1=` state permalink. Meanwhile, `computePhasedStewardshipTriage` splits the 5-year model (`computeRegenerativePracticeEconomics`) into three stages (`STAGE_1_COLLAR_AND_STABILITY_TRIAGE`, `STAGE_2_RADIAL_SOIL_SPONGE`, and `STAGE_3_GUILD_AND_STIM_AUDIT`), bounding Day-1 Stage 1 price at `Math.min(Math.round(full5YrGross * 0.24), Math.max(650, Math.round(removalBidUsd * 0.72)))` so immediate preservation care undercuts a one-time removal bid while building multi-year practitioner margin.
* **Pillar 2: Three-Layer Epistemic Separation (`docs/LAYERS.md`):** Open Arbor strictly isolates deterministic facts from uncalibrated economic hypotheses. **Layer 1 (`determination-v1.0.0`)** governs root zones, municipal ordinances, Oak Wilt windows, and Mattheck `t/R >= 0.30` gates, keeping partial-city gaps (`tx-cedar-park`, `tx-round-rock` `Threshold_And_Fee_Record` entries) visible as `UNVERIFIED`. **Layer 2 (`valuation-models-v0.3.0`)** governs `CTLA` 10th Edition replacement models (`$65/sq in` unit baseline), the 200-year holobiont biological equity hypothesis (`1.0` to `3.5` veteran keystone habitat multiplier), allometrics, and soil sponge capacity. **Layer 3 (`practice-economics-v0.2.0`)** governs phased triage and 5-year margin estimates (`computeRegenerativePracticeEconomics`, `computePhasedStewardshipTriage`). Layer 2 and Layer 3 remain `0.x` experimental models subordinate to Layer 1 determinations.
* **Pillar 3: Symbiosis Between the AI `OpenArbor RAP Steward` and Human `RAPPER` (`server/mcp/openarbor-mcp.mjs` `v2.2.0`):** One zero-key stdio MCP server exposes `openarbor_consult`, 8 specialist modules (`calculate_root_zone`, `evaluate_ordinance`, `calculate_ecosystem_benefits`, `rap_biomechanics`, `rap_lint`, `lookup_ordinance`, `validate_soil_function`, `leo_rap_verify`), and 3 built-in `LEO-RAP` prompts (`homeowner-tree-guide`, `rap-practitioner-brief`, `municipal-plan-review`) across Claude Code, OpenAI Codex CLI, Hermes, Cursor, Claude Desktop, and Antigravity (`agents/openarbor-steward/`). Because the Steward skill forbids mental math and prohibits fabricating `t/R` or lab soil values without physical instrumentation, every AI consultation channels property owners toward qualified human field diagnostics instead of displacing local fieldwork.
* **Pillar 4: Local-First, Zero-PII Watershed Cohort Logging (`src/core/fieldObservationLog.js`):** Conforming to `schemas/rap_field_observation.schema.json` (`RAP_Field_Observation_v1`), `assertZeroClientPii` and `redactPotentialPii` block 17 client PII keys (`FORBIDDEN_PII_KEYS`) and 3 prototype-pollution keys while scrubbing email addresses, phone numbers, and street addresses from free text. `coarsenPlusCode` truncates GPS Plus Codes to 6-character watershed cells (`XXXXXX00+`, roughly `1.1 km` resolution), `canonical_sha256` seals integrity, `privacy_attestation` asserts `zero_client_pii: true` and `plus_code_coarsened_to_watershed_cell: true`, and `summarizeObservationCohort` tracks cohort density toward an initial 10-consultation target (`targetCohortSize = 10`).

## 5. Four-Rung Proof-of-Restoration `RAPPER` Progression Ladder and Attestation

Rather than gating credibility behind lecture hours or academy paywalls, Open Arbor structures practitioner progression around verifiable diagnostic, rhizosphere, and longitudinal ecological outcomes:

| Rung | Role in Open Arbor | Verifiable Mechanism & Code / Schema Anchor | Renewal & Calibration Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. `RAP Observer`** | Homeowner, land steward, student, or volunteer | Runs `openarbor_consult` (`homeowner-tree-guide`), measures trunk circumference at `4.5 ft` (`DBH`), reviews city permits, and sends `steward_to_arborist_message` handoffs without unverified `t/R` claims. | Per-tree awareness; zero fee and zero credential claim. |
| **2. `Provisional RAPPER`** | Qualified field practitioner entering `RAP-Spec v2.0` | Completes 3 schema-valid, linter-clean `RAP_v2` assessments (`lintRapEvidentiaryReport`), verifies pneumatic root-collar excavation, biological amendments, and soil baseline, and sets `year_1_recovery_milestone_target_date` to mint `Verified Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol Practitioner in Ecological Restoration (RAPPER)` via `mintOpenBadgeAttestation` (`schemas/rap_openbadge_v3.schema.json`). | Optional registry maintenance benchmarked to Path C (`$75` to `$110/yr` referencing `SER CERP` `$75/yr` and `NOFA AOLCP` `$110/yr`; `payment_flow_encoded: false`). |
| **3. `Outcome-Verified RAPPER`** | Active ecological restoration practitioner | Renews annual standing by appending `longitudinal_outcomes` entries in `RAP_Field_Observation_v1` tracking Year-1 and Year-5 `twig_extension_cm`, `canopy_dieback_pct`, `infiltration_mm_hr`, and `survival_status` (`Thriving`, `Stable`, `Declining`, `Failed_Or_Removed`). | Renewal rests on measured survival and soil recovery indicators rather than passive webinar hours. |
| **4. `Steward RAPPER`** | Bioregional calibration and Ordinance Commons contributor | Exports anonymized `targetCohortSize >= 10` observation cohorts via `serializeObservationCohortJsonl` (`~1.1 km` watershed cells) and verifies municipal statutes in `data/ordinances/` (`validate-ordinance.mjs`). | Empirical outcome cohorts calibrate Layer 2 `0.x` survival and soil-sponge models and catch municipal ordinance drift. |

**Cryptographic Trust Status (`docs/13_RAP_PRACTICE_SPECIFICATION_v1.md` Section 6.2):** Currently, `mintOpenBadgeAttestation` in `src/core/rapEngine.js` signs `OpenBadgeCredential` JSON-LD artifacts locally with deterministic content integrity (`type: "DataIntegrityProof"`, `cryptosuite: "sha256-integrity"`, `verificationMethod: "https://soilgrower.com/specs/rap-spec-v2.0#sha256-content-digest"`). External `Ed25519Signature2020` issuer key custody and live revocation registry publication remain `UNVERIFIED` (placeholder status in the local static bundle) until external cohort verification infrastructure is deployed.

## 6. Open Arbor Two-Register Citation Rule

Confined strictly to Project Open Arbor documentation, reports, badges, and practitioner communications, Open Arbor uses two deliberate registers so ecological clarity never sacrifices municipal legitimacy:

1. **Register A (Statutory, Municipal, Legal, and CV Submittals):** Lead with the full unabbreviated title **Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol Practitioner in Ecological Restoration (`RAP-Spec v2.0`)**, or **Certified RAP Practitioner** on character-limited municipal permit forms. Pair this title with any applicable statutory credential recorded in `legacy_statutory_equivalencies` (`ISA_Certified_Arborist`, `ISA_Board_Certified_Master_Arborist`, `ASCA_Registered_Consulting_Arborist`, `SAF_Certified_Urban_And_Community_Forester`, `SAF_Certified_Forester`, `SER_Certified_Ecological_Restoration_Practitioner`, or `Accredited_Degree_Forestry_Arboriculture_Horticulture_Ecology`). Never abbreviate an Open Arbor practitioner as `RCA`.
2. **Register B (Field Practice, Open Badges, Guild Education, and Community Culture):** Use **`RAPPER`**, **`RAP`**, and **`"That's a RAP"`** across 1EdTech Open Badges 3.0 credential names (`Verified Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol Practitioner in Ecological Restoration (RAPPER)`), `openarbor-rap` CLI commands, `arborist_truck_summary` handoffs, field cohort logs, and practitioner guild training.

## 7. Protocol Adoption and Legibility Hypotheses (`H-RAP-1` through `H-RAP-3`)

Alongside the valuation and economics kill criteria in [docs/LAYERS.md](./LAYERS.md), Open Arbor treats the human adoption behavior of `RAP` and `RAPPER` as testable field hypotheses rather than assumed facts:

1. **`H-RAP-1` (Collaborative Handoff Reception):** Hypothesis that stripping unmeasured `t/R` defaults and contractor bid anchors from `steward_to_arborist_message` while supplying deterministic city tier and `CRZ` geometry increases cooperating field inspections on threatened mature trees. *Kill criterion:* If more than `50%` of contacted arborists across the first `10` live consultations (`targetCohortSize = 10`) refuse inspection or dismiss the brief, revise `composeArboristHandoffBrief` using exact verbatim practitioner feedback.
2. **`H-RAP-2` (Stage-1 Triage Conversion):** Hypothesis that capping `STAGE_1_COLLAR_AND_STABILITY_TRIAGE` at `<= 72%` of a competitor removal estimate increases homeowner election of root-collar diagnosis on structurally sound (`t/R >= 0.30`) specimens while maintaining positive crew operating margin. *Kill criterion:* If empirical billing across `10` live mature-tree cases yields Stage 1 acceptance below `30%` or negative operating margin after pneumatic equipment mobilization, retire the `0.24 / 0.72` ratio in `computePhasedStewardshipTriage` in favor of explicit operator labor and mobilization inputs.
3. **`H-RAP-3` (Two-Register Municipal Legibility):** Hypothesis that leading statutory filings with the full Register A title (`Regenerative Arboriculture Protocol Practitioner in Ecological Restoration (RAP-Spec v2.0)`) prevents colloquial distraction from the `RAPPER` acronym during city plan reviews and administrative hearings. *Kill criterion:* If municipal reviewers or hearing officers object to the `RAPPER` acronym inside attached `OpenBadgeCredential` evidence payloads, retain `RAPPER` strictly in Register B community artifacts and render `Certified RAP Practitioner (RAP-Spec v2.0)` on municipal export headers.
